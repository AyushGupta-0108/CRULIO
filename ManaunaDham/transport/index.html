Frequently Asked Questions
Common questions about our cab service
How much does a cab from Bareilly to Manauna Dham cost?
Starting from ₹1,599 for both 5-seater and 7-seater cabs. Best rates when you book 15+ days in advance.
How far is Manauna Dham from Bareilly?
43 km from Bareilly Railway Station and 51 km from Bareilly Airport. Drive takes about 1 hour 15–25 minutes.
How much do I pay online to confirm the cab?
Only 10% of the total fare online (plus a small platform fee). The remaining 90% is paid directly to the driver at pickup.
Is a 5-seater cab available?
Yes! Both 5-seater (Sedan/Hatchback) and 7-seater (SUV/MUV) options are available. Select your preference in step 3 of the booking form.
Is Crulio.com/ManaunaDham the official temple website?
No. Crulio.com/ManaunaDham is a privately operated pilgrim services platform by Crulio Networks. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by the temple management.
